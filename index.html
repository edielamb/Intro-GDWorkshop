Hobbies & Interests

GRADEX

Resonant Matter

Media Installation

An audio-reactive media installation displayed on a CRT television. Thousands of particles assigned XYZ coordinates that move, change, and react to audio frequencies.

Referencing the interplay between organic matter and audible stimuli. The particles move just like the molecules in our bodies vibrate.

SUBVERSITYPE

Typeface & Specimen

SUBVERSITYPE is an abstract, illegible typeface meant to undermine the illusion of authenticity in institutional messages. Each glyph is built from an equal number of bars on a grid, bar height varies to create the letterforms.

Based off the word ‘Subversive,’ the typeface and specimen booklet serve as a critique of institutional messaging, and a commentary on the future potential of type legibility.
